2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide directly.

  • Account identifiers. When you authenticate through Google or Discord we receive and store the unique user identifier assigned to you by that provider, the display name and avatar image URL associated with that account, and a record of which providers are linked to your account.
  • Profile information. A display name and profile image URL of your choosing, if you set them, and your interface preferences (theme, accent color, text size and motion setting).
  • User Content. Character sheets and everything entered into them, homebrew library entries, campaign records, campaign settings and membership, and the change history the Service keeps for finished characters.
  • Correspondence. Messages submitted through the Contact page, together with any name, Discord username or email address you choose to supply, and the date and time of submission. Where you are signed in, the message is associated with your account.

2.2 Information collected automatically.

  • Authentication tokens. A session token is generated at sign-in and stored in your browser's local storage. We retain only a SHA-256 hash of that token, together with its creation and expiry timestamps.
  • Account timestamps. The date your account was created and the date it was last used.
  • Abuse-prevention identifiers. To rate-limit the Contact form we compute and store a one-way SHA-256 hash derived from your network address or account identifier. We do not store the network address itself, and the hash cannot be reversed to recover it.
  • Server and host logs. Our hosting providers process technical data, which may include IP addresses, user-agent strings and request metadata, in order to deliver, secure and troubleshoot the Service. Such processing is governed by their respective privacy policies; we do not maintain analytics logs of our own.

2.3 Information from third parties. Google additionally transmits the email address associated with your Google account and whether it is verified. That address is evaluated at the moment of sign-in solely to determine whether the account is designated as an administrator or game master, and is then discarded; it is not written to our database. From Discord we request only the identify scope, and accordingly receive no email address, server membership or message content.

2.4 Information held only on your device. The Service may be used without an account. Characters and homebrew created in that state are written to your browser's local storage and are not transmitted to us. Clearing your browser's site data erases them irretrievably.

2.5 Categories of personal information (California). In the twelve months preceding the date of this Policy, we have collected the following statutory categories of personal information: identifiers (provider account identifier, display name, avatar URL, and any contact details you voluntarily supply); internet or other electronic network activity information (session metadata and hashed abuse-prevention identifiers); and other user-generated content described in Section 2.1. We do not collect sensitive personal information, biometric information, geolocation data, or commercial or financial information.

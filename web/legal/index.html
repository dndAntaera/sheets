Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This policy covers 3.5 Sheets at dndantaera.github.io/sheets, the character sheet site that accompanies the Antæra Wiki.

Using the sheets without signing in

You can make and keep characters without an account. Everything you enter is saved in your own browser's storage on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it.

Signing in

You may sign in with Google or Discord. We never see or store a password. When you sign in we receive, and keep:

  • the user id Google or Discord gives your account, so we recognise you next time
  • your display name and the address of your avatar picture
  • which of the two you have linked to your account
  • your role on the site (player, GM or admin), and when you signed up and last signed in
  • a username and profile picture, if you choose your own, and how you like the site to look (theme, accent color, text size and motion)

Google also sends your email address. It is checked once, at sign-in, against the site's list of administrators, and is not stored. From Discord we ask only for the identify permission; we do not receive your email, your servers or your messages.

What you save

While signed in, the following are stored on our server under your account:

  • your characters, including anything you type into them
  • homebrew you create (races, classes, feats and the like), in a library of your own
  • if you run a campaign, the homebrew you write for it
  • campaigns you create or join, their settings and house rules, and your membership in them

Who can see it

  • Your characters and homebrew are visible only to you, except as described next.
  • Your username and profile picture can be seen by people in campaigns you share, and by site admins. Your profile shows them the campaigns you share, and nothing you keep.
  • Homebrew written for a campaign can be seen by everyone in that campaign, and changed by its GMs.
  • A character you add to a campaign can be seen and edited by that campaign's GMs, and seen by its other players if the campaign allows it. Take the character out of the campaign and they lose that access.
  • Site admins can see the list of accounts (names, roles, and how many characters, homebrew entries and campaigns each holds) in order to manage them. The site does not show admins the contents of anyone's homebrew library.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sessions and cookies

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. When you sign in, your browser keeps a sign-in token in its local storage; our server stores only a scrambled (hashed) form of it. Sessions last 30 days, or until you sign out.

Where it is kept

The site is served by GitHub Pages, and the server and its database run on Cloudflare Workers and D1. Like any web host, they may process technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the service, under their own privacy policies. Sign-in is handled by Google or Discord under their own.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete any of your characters or homebrew at any time from the site, and signing out removes the sign-in token from your browser. To have your whole account deleted, along with everything stored under it, contact us as below; an admin will remove it. Characters of other players in campaigns you own are released back to their owners, not deleted.

Children

The sheets are not directed at children under 13, and you must meet the minimum age Google or Discord sets for its accounts in order to sign in.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

For questions or to delete your account, open an issue at github.com/dndAntaera/sheets/issues or contact the Antæra Dungeon Master.
